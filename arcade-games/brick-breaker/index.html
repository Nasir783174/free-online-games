How to Play Brick Breaker

Brick Breaker is a simple arcade game where you control a paddle at the bottom of the screen. The ball bounces around the game board and destroys bricks when it hits them. Your goal is to clear every brick without losing all of your lives.

Controls

On desktop, use the Left and Right arrow keys or the A and D keys to move the paddle. You can also use your mouse. On mobile devices, use the large left and right buttons below the game board.

Scoring

Each brick you destroy gives you points. Clear a complete level to advance to the next one. Higher levels contain more bricks and make the ball move faster.

Brick Breaker Tips and Strategy

Try to keep the paddle underneath the ball instead of moving too far from side to side. Watch the ball's direction carefully and position the paddle before it reaches the bottom of the board. Breaking bricks near the sides can also create useful angles that help clear several bricks in succession.

About Brick Breaker

Brick Breaker is a classic arcade-style game built around a paddle, bouncing ball, and rows of breakable blocks. The basic gameplay is easy to understand, but later levels require faster reactions and careful paddle control.

This browser version is designed for quick play on desktop and mobile devices. The game runs entirely in your browser, so there is no account, download, or server-side processing required.

Brick Breaker FAQ

Can I play Brick Breaker online for free?

Yes. This Brick Breaker game can be played directly in your browser without downloading additional software.

How do I move the paddle?

Use the Left and Right arrow keys, A and D keys, your mouse, or the on-screen controls on supported mobile devices.

What happens when I lose the ball?

You lose one life. If you still have lives remaining, the ball is reset and you can continue playing.

How do I reach the next level?

Destroy every brick on the current level. The next level then starts with a larger brick layout and increased ball speed.
