Mission controls

How to Play Space Shooter

Your starfighter fires automatically while the mission is active. Keep moving, line up with enemy ships, and survive every attack pattern. A wave ends when all enemies are destroyed.

Move

Use Arrow keys or WASD. You can also guide the ship with a mouse or finger.

Attack

Line up your ship with targets. Hold Space for extra rapid shots when your weapon is ready.

Survive

Avoid enemy ships and red plasma shots. Losing all three lives ends the mission.

Power up

Collect blue energy cores for rapid fire and green repair cores to recover a life.

About the game

Free Space Shooter Online

This browser game is a compact arcade shoot ’em up built for quick sessions. Each wave introduces faster movement, tougher enemies, and denser attack patterns. Your score rises for every target destroyed, with bonus points for clearing waves and finishing the mission.

Everything runs locally in your browser using HTML5 Canvas. No download, login, or installation is required.

Space Shooter Tips

  • Stay near the lower half of the field for more reaction time.
  • Watch enemy shots instead of staring only at your ship.
  • Target tougher purple ships early before the screen gets busy.
  • Save rapid fire for dense waves and the command ship.

Quick answers

Space Shooter FAQ

How do I play the Space Shooter game?

Move with Arrow keys, WASD, a mouse, or touch. Your ship fires automatically, and Space activates extra rapid shots. Destroy all five waves while avoiding enemy fire.

Can I play Space Shooter on mobile?

Yes. Drag your finger inside the game area or use the large on-screen direction buttons. Automatic firing makes the game easy to control on phones and tablets.

Is this Space Shooter free to play?

Yes. It is a free online game that runs directly in a modern browser without a download, account, or installation.

How is the score calculated?

Enemy ships award points based on their strength. You also earn a wave-clear bonus and a larger mission-complete bonus. Your best score is stored locally on your device.
