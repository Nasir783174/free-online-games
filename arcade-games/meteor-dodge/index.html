Meteor Dodge

Play a free avoidance game online. Dodge meteors, collect stars, and chase a high score in your browser with no download required.

How to Play This Avoidance Game

Move the blue ship around the game area and stay clear of every meteor. Your score rises while you survive. Gold stars are optional bonuses worth 100 points, while close escapes earn near-miss points.

  1. Start moving. Use the arrow keys or WASD on desktop. On mobile, drag on the game area or use the direction buttons.
  2. Watch the whole field. Meteors enter from above and become faster as your level increases.
  3. Protect your lives. A collision removes one life and briefly makes your ship invulnerable. The run ends after three hits.
  4. Collect stars wisely. Stars improve your score, but chasing one through a crowded area may not be worth the risk.

About Avoidance Games

Avoidance games test reaction time, movement, and spatial awareness. The goal is simple: stay away from hazards for as long as possible. Meteor Dodge adds collectible stars, near-miss rewards, and steadily increasing difficulty to make each run different without adding complicated rules.

This free online avoidance game runs entirely in your browser. There is no download, account, or sign-up. Your best score is stored only in your current browser.

Avoidance Game FAQ

Is Meteor Dodge free to play?

Yes. The game is free to play in a modern browser and does not require a download or account.

Can I play this avoidance game on mobile?

Yes. You can drag your finger on the game area, swipe, or use the large on-screen direction buttons.

How does the score work?

You earn points for survival time, 100 points for each star, and bonus points for passing close to a meteor without touching it.

Does the game save my best score?

Yes. The best score is stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser storage will reset it.
