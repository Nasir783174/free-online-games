How to Play Endless Runner

Your runner moves forward automatically. Jump at the right moment to clear crates and barriers. Collect floating coins for bonus points and stay alive as the running speed increases.

  1. Start the run. Select Start Run or press Space.
  2. Time your jumps. Jump once from the ground and once more in the air for a double jump.
  3. Avoid obstacles. Hitting any barrier ends the run.
  4. Collect coins. Each coin adds 25 bonus points.

Game Controls Jump / Double Jump Space ↑ Pause / Resume P Restart after game over R On a phone or tablet, use the large Jump button. You can also tap the game area to jump.

About the Endless Runner Game

Endless Runner is a fast browser arcade game built around timing and concentration. The route continues for as long as you can avoid hazards. Speed rises gradually, so each run becomes more challenging without changing the simple controls.

The game runs entirely in your browser using HTML5. There is no account, installation, or download required. Your best score is saved only in your current browser.

Tips for a Higher Score

  • Watch the space in front of the runner instead of focusing only on the character.
  • Save the second jump for tall barriers or a late correction.
  • Do not chase a coin if collecting it puts you on course to hit an obstacle.
  • Use short, deliberate taps. Holding the key does not create extra jumps.

Endless Runner FAQ

Is Endless Runner free to play?

Yes. This Endless Runner game is free to play online in a modern web browser.

Do I need to download the game?

No. The game loads and runs directly in your browser without an app or game download.

Can I play Endless Runner on mobile?

Yes. Use the on-screen Jump button or tap the game area on a phone or tablet.

How is the score calculated?

Your score rises with the distance you run. Every collected coin adds 25 bonus points.
