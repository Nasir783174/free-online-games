Shooting

Play Shooting Game free online in your browser. Aim, hit moving targets, build combos, and chase a high score with mouse, touch, or keyboard controls. No download required.

How to Play Shooting Game

Start the round and aim at the colored targets moving across the range. Shoot a target before it leaves the game area to earn points. Smaller targets are worth more, and consecutive hits increase your combo multiplier.

Controls

  • Desktop: Move the mouse and click a target to shoot.
  • Keyboard: Use Arrow keys or WASD to move the crosshair and Space to fire.
  • Mobile: Tap directly on a target, or use the optional on-screen controls.

Shooting Game Tips

  • Prioritize small targets because they award more points.
  • Keep consecutive shots accurate to grow your combo.
  • Watch targets near the edges so they do not escape.
  • A missed shot breaks your combo, so accuracy matters.

About This Shooting Game

This free online shooting game is a lightweight arcade target challenge built for quick browser play. Each round lasts up to 60 seconds. Targets vary in size, speed, direction, and point value, so the difficulty increases as your score rises.

No download or account is required. The game runs entirely in your browser and supports desktop and mobile controls.

Shooting Game FAQ

Is this shooting game free?

Yes. You can play the game free in a modern web browser without downloading game software.

Can I play the shooting game on mobile?

Yes. Tap directly on targets to shoot, or use the on-screen directional and fire controls.
