How to Play Bullet Hell

Control the player ship around the arena and fire continuously or whenever you have a clear target. Enemy ships enter from the top and sides, then launch patterns of projectiles. Stay alert, use open gaps, and avoid getting trapped near the edges.

  • Move: Use WASD or the arrow keys. On mobile, use the on-screen directional controls.
  • Shoot: Hold Space on desktop or the center touch button on mobile.
  • Dodge: Enemy bullets are the main threat. Your ship's collision point is intentionally small for precise dodging.
  • Survive: Waves become more challenging over time. Defeating enemies earns points and helps you build a higher score.

Bullet Hell Tips

  • Watch the spaces between bullet patterns instead of focusing on individual projectiles.
  • Keep moving, but avoid making large panic movements when a small adjustment is enough.
  • Clear enemies early when they create crowded patterns.
  • Near the end of a wave, position yourself where the next group has room to spawn.

About the Game

Bullet Hell is a fast arcade survival game built with HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript. It runs entirely in your browser with no account, backend, or download required.

Bullet Hell FAQ

Can I play Bullet Hell on mobile?

Yes. The game includes touch controls designed for smaller screens.

Is Bullet Hell free to play?

Yes. This browser version is designed to be played for free without a download or account.

How is the score calculated?

Destroying enemies awards points. Surviving also increases your score over time, with later waves providing a stronger challenge.
