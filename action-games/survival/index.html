How to Play Survival Game

Your goal is simple: stay alive and build the highest score possible. Move around the wilderness, collect useful supplies, and keep your distance from enemies. Your score rises while you survive and increases faster when you collect food.

Survival Game Controls

On desktop, move with the arrow keys or W, A, S, and D. Press P to pause or resume. On phones and tablets, use the large on-screen directional controls below the game area.

About Survival Game

This browser survival game is built around movement, resource collection, and escalating pressure. Each wave introduces more danger, so positioning becomes more important the longer your run lasts. The game works entirely in your browser with no account, download, or server-side processing.

Survival Tips

Do not trap yourself near the edge of the map. Collect medkits when your health is low, use open space to circle enemies, and plan a safe route before chasing food for extra points.
