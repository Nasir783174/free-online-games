How to Play Platform Action

Move through the level by jumping between platforms, collecting coins, and avoiding hazards. You can defeat an enemy by landing on top of it. Reach the portal at the far right to win.

Controls

  • Move: Arrow keys or A and D
  • Jump: Space, W, or Up Arrow
  • Mobile: Use the on-screen left, right, and jump buttons
  • Restart: Use the Restart button after a mistake or completed run

Tips and Strategy

Keep moving, but do not rush every jump. Watch enemy movement, use wide platforms to line up difficult jumps, and collect coins when the route is safe. A jump onto an enemy is safer than running directly into it.
