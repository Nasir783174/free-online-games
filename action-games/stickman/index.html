Stickman

Play Stickman Game free online in your browser. Run, jump, avoid obstacles, collect coins, and survive as long as you can with no download.

How to Play Stickman Game

Control the running stickman and keep moving through the course. Jump over obstacles and collect coins whenever you can. The longer you survive, the higher your score becomes.

Controls

  • Space or Arrow Up — Jump
  • Jump button — Touch control on mobile
  • Press jump again after landing to keep your run going.

Stickman Game Tips

  • Watch the road ahead rather than focusing only on your score.
  • Coins are useful, but do not risk a collision for a difficult coin.
  • As the run gets faster, react early and use short, controlled jumps.

About Stickman Game

Stickman Game is a lightweight browser runner built with HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript. It is designed for quick play on desktop and mobile devices without downloads, accounts, or server-side game processing.

Stickman Game FAQ

Can I play Stickman Game online for free?

Yes. The game runs directly in a modern web browser and does not require a download.

How do I jump?

Press Space or Arrow Up on a keyboard. On mobile, use the Jump button.

What is the goal?

Survive as long as possible, avoid obstacles, collect coins, and build the highest score you can.
