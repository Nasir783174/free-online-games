How to Play Zombie Game

Your character starts near the center while the base sits behind you. Zombies enter from the edges of the arena and move toward the base. Move around the play area, aim toward the threat, and shoot before zombies get close.

  • Use WASD or the arrow keys to move.
  • Use your mouse to aim and click to fire on desktop.
  • On mobile, use the movement pad and the FIRE button.
  • Keep zombies away from the base. If its health reaches zero, the game ends.
  • Each defeated zombie adds to your score. Surviving longer increases the wave difficulty.

Zombie Game Tips

Keep Moving

Standing still makes it easier for groups of zombies to surround you.

Protect the Base

Prioritize zombies that are closest to the base when several enemies approach together.

Watch the Wave

Later waves send tougher and faster zombies, so keep your position flexible.

About This Zombie Game

This is a lightweight browser-based zombie survival game built with HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript. It runs entirely on the client side, so you can play without an account, database, backend, or download.
