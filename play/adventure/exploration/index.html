Guide

How to Play Exploration

Move through the island one tile at a time and uncover the map around your explorer. Your goal is to collect five blue relics and return them to the golden temple in the northeast corner.

  1. 1
    Reveal the island

    Walk through the mist to discover safe paths, ruins, fruit, and hazards.

  2. 2
    Recover five relics

    Each relic increases your score and moves you closer to opening the temple.

  3. 3
    Reach the temple

    Once every relic is found, enter the temple gate before your time or energy runs out.

Explorer's notes

Controls & Strategy

  • Use arrow keys or WASD on a computer.
  • Use the large direction buttons on phones and tablets.
  • Collect green fruit to restore 18 energy.
  • Avoid thorn patches; each hit costs extra energy.
  • Relics farther from camp are worth more points.
  • Open terrain costs less energy than dense jungle.

About the game

What Is the Exploration Game?

Exploration is a short browser adventure built around discovery and route planning. Each expedition creates a new island layout, so relic locations, jungle paths, hazards, and energy fruit change whenever you start a new map.

The game combines a fog-of-war map with light survival mechanics. There is no download or account: the complete adventure runs in your browser using HTML5.

Common questions

Exploration Game FAQ

Is the Exploration game free to play?

Yes. Exploration is a free online game that runs directly in a modern web browser.

Can I play Exploration on a phone?

Yes. The game includes responsive touch controls for phones and tablets, as well as keyboard controls for desktop players.

How do I win the game?

Collect all five relics and enter the golden temple before the three-minute timer ends or your explorer runs out of energy.

Does each expedition use the same map?

No. A fresh island is generated whenever you choose New Map or replay the game.
