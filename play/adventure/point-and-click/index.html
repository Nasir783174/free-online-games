How to Play This Point & Click Game

Click or tap objects in the room to inspect them. Some discoveries become inventory items. Select an item, then choose the object where you want to use it. Follow clues, enter the correct code, and open the observatory to finish the adventure.

About The Clockwork Study

The Clockwork Study is a short, complete point-and-click adventure inspired by classic room-escape games. Instead of fast reactions, it rewards observation and connecting clues. Your score begins at 1,000 points, with bonuses for hidden tokens and small penalties for hints or incorrect puzzle attempts.

Point & Click Adventure Tips

Examine a clue more than once if its meaning is not immediately clear. Inventory items are only useful in logical places, and the symbol order matters as much as the numbers you discover. If you are stuck, check whether a selected item is highlighted before clicking its target.

Point & Click Game FAQ

Is this point-and-click game free?

Yes. The full adventure is free to play in your browser, with no account or download required.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Every interactive object uses touch-friendly controls, and the game layout adapts to smaller screens.

How long does the adventure take?

A first playthrough usually takes about 10–20 minutes, depending on how quickly you connect the clues.

Does the game save progress?

Your current run is kept in the same browser, so an accidental refresh will not erase your progress. Restart clears the active run.
