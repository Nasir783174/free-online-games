How to Play Platform Adventure

Guide your explorer across floating ledges, forest ruins, and mountain paths. Each stage contains golden coins and crystal relics. Coins increase your score, while every relic in the stage must be collected before the exit portal will open.

Jump on a guardian to defeat it, or avoid the guardian entirely. Falling into water, touching spikes, or colliding with a guardian from the side costs one life. Activated checkpoints let you continue from a safer point in the stage.

Desktop Controls

  • A D or arrow keys to move
  • W, ↑, or Space to jump
  • P or Esc to pause

Mobile Controls

  • Hold the left or right button to run
  • Tap Jump to leap
  • Use the pause button above the game

Your Objective

  • Collect every crystal relic
  • Find coins for bonus points
  • Reach the portal with lives remaining

About Platform Adventure

Platform Adventure is a free online platform game built for quick browser play. The journey spans three increasingly challenging stages, each with a distinct route, moving hazards, hidden pickups, and a checkpoint. No download or account is needed, and your best score is saved on your device.

Tips for a Higher Score

Keep moving

Finishing quickly adds a time bonus at the end of each stage.

Explore high paths

Some coins and relics sit above the most obvious route.

Time your jumps

Watch enemy patrols and moving platforms before committing.

Use checkpoints

Touch the trail flag so a missed jump does not send you far back.

Platform Adventure FAQ

Is Platform Adventure free to play?

Yes. Platform Adventure is a free browser game with no download, login, or purchase required.

Can I play Platform Adventure on mobile?

Yes. The game includes responsive scaling and large touch controls for compatible phones and tablets.

How do I open the exit portal?

Collect every crystal relic in the current stage. The portal changes from dim to glowing when it is ready.

Does the game save my progress?

Your best score is stored locally in your browser. A new session begins at stage one.
