Your mission

How to Play Quest

Guide the ranger through the ruins and collect all three glowing sun relics. The exit portal stays sealed until every relic is recovered. Coins increase your score, hearts restore health, and shadow creatures can be defeated with your sword.

  1. Explore the map. Move around walls, trees, water, and broken pillars.
  2. Recover three relics. Each relic adds 250 points and brings the portal closer to opening.
  3. Survive the shadows. Attack nearby enemies or move carefully around them.
  4. Reach the portal. Enter it after collecting every relic to complete the quest.

A short browser adventure

About the Quest Game

Quest is a top-down adventure game built for quick sessions. It combines maze-like exploration, light combat, collectible hunting, and a clear final objective. The entire game runs in your browser, so there is no download, account, or installation.

The ruins reset each time you replay, letting you improve your route, protect more health, defeat more enemies, and chase a higher score.

Quick answers

Quest Game FAQ

Is Quest free to play online?

Yes. Quest is a free browser game with no download, signup, or account required.

Can I play Quest on a phone?

Yes. The game includes large touch controls and swipe movement for modern mobile browsers.

How do I win the Quest game?

Collect all three sun relics and then move onto the golden portal. You lose if your health reaches zero.

How is the score calculated?

You earn points for coins, relics, and defeated enemies. Completing the quest also awards remaining-health and time bonuses.
