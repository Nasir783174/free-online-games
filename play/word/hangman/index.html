How to Play Hangman

Try to uncover the hidden word by selecting letters. Correct guesses reveal every matching letter. An incorrect guess adds another part to the hangman drawing and reduces your remaining guesses.

  • Choose a letter using the on-screen keyboard or your physical keyboard.
  • Each incorrect letter counts as one mistake.
  • Reveal the complete word before all six mistakes are used.
  • Use the Hint button once per round to reveal a letter for a small score cost.

About Hangman

Hangman is a classic word-guessing game built around vocabulary, spelling, and deduction. This browser version uses themed word sets and gives you a fresh puzzle each round without requiring a download or account.

Hangman Tips

Start with common letters such as E, T, A, O, I, N, S, and R. Look at the word pattern as letters appear, and use the category as a clue rather than guessing randomly.

Hangman Game FAQ

Can I play Hangman online for free?

Yes. This Hangman game runs directly in a modern web browser with no download or account required.

How many wrong guesses do I get?

You can make up to six incorrect guesses in each round before the game ends.

Can I use my keyboard?

Yes. Click the on-screen letters or press A–Z on a physical keyboard.
