How to Play Connect Four

Connect Four is played on a seven-column, six-row board. Red moves first. Players then alternate turns, dropping one disc into any column that still has space. Gravity places the disc in the lowest open slot.

The round ends when one player forms an unbroken line of four matching discs. The line can run across, up and down, or diagonally. If all 42 spaces fill without a winning line, the round is a draw.

Connect Four Strategy Tips

◎

Control the center

Middle columns participate in more possible four-disc lines, giving your pieces more ways to connect.

◇

Watch every threat

Before each move, check whether your opponent can win on the next turn and block that space first.

↗

Create two options

A strong setup produces two winning spaces at once, so your opponent cannot block both with one disc.

About the Connect Four Game

Connect Four combines simple rules with meaningful strategy. Each move changes the spaces available above it, so a helpful-looking disc can also create an opening for the other player. Quick answers

Connect Four FAQ

Can I play Connect Four online for free?

Yes. This Connect Four game is free to play in your browser and does not require a download, signup, or account.

How do you win Connect Four?

Win by placing four discs of your color in one continuous horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line before the other player.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Tap a column arrow or the board to drop a disc. The game board automatically resizes for smaller screens.

What happens when the board is full?

If every space is filled and neither player has connected four discs, the round is recorded as a draw.
