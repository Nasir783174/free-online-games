Rules and controls

How to Play Checkers

Checkers is played by two people on the dark squares of an 8×8 board. Red moves first. Select a piece, then select a highlighted destination. On a phone or tablet, simply tap the piece and square.

↗

Move diagonally

Regular pieces move one dark square diagonally toward the opposite side.

⇥

Capture pieces

Jump over an adjacent opponent into an empty square. If a capture is available, you must take it.

♛

Crown a king

Reach the far edge to become a king. Kings can move and capture both forward and backward.

×2

Chain your jumps

After a capture, keep jumping with the same piece when another capture is available.

How do you win?

Capture all opposing pieces or block your opponent so they cannot make a legal move. The game also declares a draw after repeated positions or a long period without captures or promotions.

Improve your play

Checkers Tips and Strategy

  • Protect your back row. Keeping a piece there can delay your opponent from earning a king.
  • Move pieces together. Connected pieces are harder to capture than isolated ones.
  • Plan forced captures. A sacrifice can pull an opposing piece into position for a multi-jump.
  • Value the center. Central pieces usually have more movement options and support both sides.

A timeless board game

About the Checkers Game

Checkers, also called English draughts, is a two-player strategy game built around diagonal movement, compulsory captures, and promotion to kings. The rules are simple enough to learn quickly, while combinations and forced jumps give experienced players room to plan ahead.

This browser version follows American Checkers rules: uncrowned pieces move and capture forward, captures are mandatory, reaching the king row ends the move, and a king can move in both directions.

Common questions

Checkers Game FAQ

Is this Checkers game free?

Yes. You can play the complete Checkers game free in your browser without creating an account or downloading an app.

Can I play Checkers on mobile?

Yes. The board scales to your screen, and all moves work with simple tap controls on modern phones and tablets.

Are captures mandatory?

Yes. When one of your pieces can capture, the game highlights only legal capturing pieces and moves.

Can I play against the computer?

This version is designed for two players sharing the same device. It does not include a computer opponent.
