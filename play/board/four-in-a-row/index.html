Simple rules, tactical choices

How to Play Four in a Row

Four in a Row is played on a seven-column, six-row grid. Players take turns dropping one disc into a column. The disc falls to the lowest available space in that column.

  1. Choose a column and drop your disc.
  2. Your opponent takes the next turn.
  3. Keep building lines while blocking your opponent.
  4. Connect four matching discs in a straight line to win.

A winning line can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal. If all 42 spaces fill without a four-disc line, the round ends in a draw.

Quick controls

  • Tap or click: choose any open column.
  • 1–7: drop a disc in that numbered column.
  • ← / →: move the keyboard column selector.
  • Enter / Space: drop into the selected column.

Strategy

Tips for Winning Four in a Row

The center columns usually create more connection possibilities than the outer edges. Strong play balances building your own threats with stopping immediate winning moves from your opponent.

  • Control the center whenever possible.
  • Look one move ahead for instant wins and blocks.
  • Create two possible winning moves at the same time.
  • Be careful when your move gives the opponent a winning landing space.

About the game

What Is Four in a Row?

Four in a Row is a two-player connection game built around gravity, pattern recognition, and short tactical sequences. Its rules are easy to learn, while stronger play depends on controlling key spaces and planning several turns ahead.

This browser version keeps the standard 7×6 board and lets you switch between local two-player mode and a computer opponent without installing anything.

How do you win Four in a Row?

Can I play Four in a Row on mobile?

Yes. The board is responsive and each column can be selected with a tap on modern mobile browsers.

Does this game require a download?

No. The game runs directly in the browser using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Can two people play on the same device?

Yes. Choose the 2 Players mode and take turns on the same screen.
