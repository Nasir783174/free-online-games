How to Play 2048

Move every tile across the four-by-four board. When two tiles with the same number meet, they combine into one tile with double the value. Each successful move adds a new 2 or 4 tile.

  1. 1
    Choose a direction

    Use the arrow keys, WASD, swipe gestures, or the on-screen arrow buttons.

  2. 2
    Combine equal tiles

    Match 2 with 2, 4 with 4, and continue building larger values.

  3. 3
    Reach 2048

    Create the 2048 tile to win. You can then keep playing for a higher score.

About the 2048 Game

2048 is a single-player sliding tile puzzle built around powers of two. The rules are simple, but every move changes the available space. A strong game balances short-term combinations with enough empty cells for future tiles.

This browser version runs entirely on your device. There is no download, login, or account required, and your best score is saved locally in your browser.

2048 Game FAQ

How do you win the 2048 game?

You win by sliding and combining equal tiles until you create a tile with the value 2048. This version lets you continue after winning if you want to chase a higher score.

Can I play 2048 on a phone?

Yes. Swipe up, down, left, or right on the board, or use the large on-screen arrow controls below it.

Does this 2048 game need a download?

No. The game runs directly in a modern web browser using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

How is the 2048 score calculated?

When two equal tiles merge, their new value is added to your score. For example, merging two 8 tiles creates 16 and adds 16 points.
