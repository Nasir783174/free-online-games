How to Play Maze Puzzle

Your goal is simple: guide the player from the purple starting point to the teal exit. Each maze is generated in your browser, so a new maze gives you a different route to solve.

  1. Start at the purple marker. Look for open passages before making your first move.
  2. Navigate the maze. Use Arrow keys or WASD on desktop. On mobile, swipe across the maze or use the on-screen direction buttons.
  3. Collect stars if you want a higher score. Stars are placed in reachable maze cells and are not required to finish.
  4. Reach the teal exit. Your final score rewards faster finishes, fewer moves, harder difficulty, and collected stars.

About Maze Puzzle

Maze Puzzle is a lightweight browser maze game built around spatial reasoning, route planning, and quick decision-making. The maze is generated locally with JavaScript, so you can immediately create another puzzle without an account, download, or server-side game processing.

Maze Puzzle Tips and Strategy

01

Scan Before Moving

Use junctions as landmarks and avoid repeatedly entering passages you already know are dead ends.

02

Follow a Wall

Keeping one side against a continuous wall is a useful fallback when you lose track of your route.

03

Balance Stars and Speed

A nearby star can improve your score, but a long detour may cost more time and moves than it is worth.

Maze Puzzle FAQ

Is Maze Puzzle free to play?

Yes. This Maze Puzzle runs directly in a modern web browser and does not require a download or account.

Can I play Maze Puzzle on mobile?

Yes. You can swipe on the maze or use the large on-screen direction controls on supported touch devices.

Are the mazes always the same?

No. A new randomized maze is generated whenever you start a new maze or change the difficulty.

How is the Maze Puzzle score calculated?

Your score is based on difficulty, maze size, collected stars, elapsed time, and the number of moves used to reach the exit.
