    About Word Puzzle

    This free online Word Puzzle is a browser-based word search game. Each puzzle hides a themed set of words inside a grid of letters. Words can appear forward or backward across rows, columns, and diagonals.

    No download or account is required. Start a new puzzle whenever you want a different grid.

    Word Puzzle Tips

    • Scan for uncommon first letters before reading every row.
    • Check all eight directions from a promising letter.
    • Longer words are often easier to confirm once you spot a distinctive sequence.
    • Use the word list to eliminate words you have already found.

    Word Puzzle FAQ

    Is this Word Puzzle free to play?

    Yes. The game runs directly in your browser and does not require a download or account.

    Does the Word Puzzle work on mobile?

    Yes. The letter grid and controls resize for phones and tablets, and letters can be selected by tapping.

    How is the score calculated?

    You earn points for every correctly found word, with extra points for longer words and remaining time when you complete the puzzle.
