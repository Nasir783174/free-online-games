How to Play Sudoku

Your goal is to complete the 9 × 9 grid so each number from 1 through 9 appears once in every row, column, and 3 × 3 box.

  1. Select any empty square.
  2. Choose a number from the pad or your keyboard.
  3. Use Notes to record possible answers without committing.
  4. Correct every square to complete the puzzle.

Three incorrect entries end the game. A hint fills the selected square correctly and can be used up to three times.

Sudoku Tips

  • Start with rows, columns, or boxes that are nearly complete.
  • Scan for a number that can fit in only one square.
  • Use notes when two or more candidates remain.
  • Recheck the crossing row and column before entering a number.

About Sudoku

Sudoku is a number-placement logic puzzle. Despite using digits, it requires no arithmetic. Each puzzle is solved through elimination and pattern recognition. This browser version creates a new playable puzzle for every game and saves your current board on the same device if you refresh the page.

Sudoku FAQ

Is this Sudoku game free?

Yes. You can play unlimited Sudoku puzzles online for free, with no account or download.

Does a Sudoku puzzle involve math?

No. The numbers are symbols. You solve the grid using logic, elimination, and pattern recognition rather than calculation.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The board, number pad, notes, erase, and hint controls are designed for both touchscreen and desktop play.

What happens if I refresh the page?

Your active puzzle, elapsed time, mistakes, notes, and progress are saved in your browser and restored on the same device.
