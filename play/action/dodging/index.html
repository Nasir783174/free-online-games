How to Play Dodging Game

Control the player at the bottom of the game area and move left or right to avoid the obstacles falling from above. Each obstacle you successfully avoid adds to your score.

About Dodging Game

Dodging is a fast reaction game built around one simple idea: stay out of danger. Obstacles become more frequent and faster as your score increases, so careful movement matters as much as quick reactions.

Tips for a Higher Score

  • Watch the whole play area instead of focusing on one obstacle.
  • Keep some room around your player so you can change direction quickly.
  • Make small movements when the path is tight.
  • Stay calm as the obstacle speed increases.

Dodging Game FAQ

Can I play Dodging Game on mobile?

Yes. The game includes large touch controls for moving left and right.

Is Dodging Game free to play?

Yes. It runs directly in your browser with no download or account required.

How is the score calculated?

Your score increases whenever you successfully avoid an obstacle. The challenge also increases over time.
