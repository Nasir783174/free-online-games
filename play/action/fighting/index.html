How to Play Fighting Game

Your goal is to reduce the CPU fighter's health to zero before your own health runs out or the round timer expires. Win two rounds to win the match. Keep moving, choose the right attack range, and block when the opponent commits to a strike.

Movement & Defense

Use A and D to move, W to jump, and L to block. Blocking reduces incoming damage, but it slows your movement and cannot be held forever without giving up pressure.

Attacks

J throws a quick punch. K delivers a slower, stronger kick. I launches a special strike after its cooldown is ready. Attacks only connect when your opponent is within range.

Mobile Controls

On phones and tablets, use the large on-screen movement and attack buttons. Hold the left, right, or block button and tap attacks just as you would with a keyboard.

About Fighting Game

This free online fighting game is a lightweight arcade-style duel built for modern browsers. It uses client-side HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so the match runs directly on your device without a download, account, database or server-side game processing.

Tips to Win

  • Use punches at close range because they recover quickly.
  • Kick when you have space to trade speed for extra damage.
  • Block predictable CPU attacks, then counter during recovery.
  • Save the special attack for a clean opening instead of wasting it out of range.

Fighting Game FAQ

Is this Fighting Game free to play?

Yes. The game is designed to run free in a modern web browser without requiring a download or account.

Can I play the Fighting Game on mobile?

Yes. The arena scales to smaller screens and includes touch controls for movement, jumping, blocking and attacks.

How do I win a match?

Win two rounds before the CPU. A round is won by reducing the opponent to zero health or having more health when the timer reaches zero.
