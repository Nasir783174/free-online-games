How to Play Solitaire

The goal of Klondike Solitaire is to move all 52 cards to four foundation piles, one for each suit. Each foundation starts with an Ace and builds upward in suit to the King.

  1. Build tableau columns downward in alternating red and black colors.
  2. Move face-up card sequences between tableau columns when the sequence is valid.
  3. Move Kings, or sequences beginning with a King, into empty tableau columns.
  4. Use the stock pile to reveal cards into the waste pile.
  5. Move Aces to foundations, then continue building each suit upward from 2 through King.

Solitaire Controls

On desktop, drag cards with your mouse or click a card and then click a destination. On touch devices, tap to select and tap again to move.

  • Stock: click or tap to draw one card.
  • Cards: drag or tap a face-up card to select it.
  • Foundation: double-click a valid top card to send it automatically.
  • Undo: restore the previous move.
  • Hint: highlight one legal move when available.

About Klondike Solitaire

Klondike is the best-known form of Solitaire. The game combines planning, sequencing, suit building, and careful use of the stock pile. This version uses a draw-one rule and allows the waste pile to be recycled when the stock is empty.

Solitaire Tips and Strategy

  • Reveal hidden tableau cards whenever you can.
  • Avoid filling an empty tableau column unless the move improves access to hidden cards.
  • Do not always move cards to foundations immediately if they are still useful for building tableau sequences.
  • Use Undo to recover from an accidental move, not as a substitute for planning.
