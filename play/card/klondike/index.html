How to Play Klondike Solitaire

The goal of Klondike Solitaire is to move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles. Each foundation holds one suit and must be built upward from Ace through King.

  1. Build tableau columns downward in alternating red and black suits.
  2. Move a King, or a valid stack beginning with a King, into an empty tableau column.
  3. Turn over exposed face-down tableau cards whenever the card above them is moved away.
  4. Use the stock pile to reveal more playable cards in the waste pile.
  5. Move Aces to foundations first, then build each suit upward to King.

About Klondike Solitaire

Klondike is one of the best-known solitaire card games. The standard layout begins with seven tableau columns, with one additional card dealt to each successive column. Only the top card in each column starts face up. The remaining cards form the stock, which supplies cards to the waste pile.

This browser version uses a one-card draw system and unlimited stock recycling. That keeps the rules familiar while making the game convenient to play on desktop and mobile devices without a download.

Klondike Solitaire Tips

  • Reveal hidden tableau cards before making low-value foundation moves.
  • Open empty tableau columns carefully because only Kings can occupy them.
  • Try to keep both red and black options available when building long tableau sequences.
  • Move Aces and low cards to foundations when doing so does not block a needed tableau move.
  • Cycle the stock with a plan rather than automatically moving every playable waste card.

Klondike Solitaire FAQ

Is this Klondike Solitaire free to play?

Yes. The game runs directly in your browser and does not require an account, download, or payment.

Can I play Klondike Solitaire on mobile?

Yes. The layout adapts to smaller screens and supports tap-to-select and tap-to-move controls.

What cards can move to an empty tableau column?

Only a King, or a correctly ordered tableau stack that begins with a King, can move into an empty column.

How do I win?

You win by moving every card to the four foundation piles, with each suit arranged from Ace through King.
