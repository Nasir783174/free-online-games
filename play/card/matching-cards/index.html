How to Play Matching Cards

The goal is to uncover every pair on the board using as few moves as possible. A move is counted whenever you reveal a second card.

  1. Select one face-down card to reveal its symbol.
  2. Select a second card and compare the two symbols.
  3. If they match, both cards remain face up.
  4. If they do not match, they flip back after a short pause.
  5. Continue until all eight pairs are matched.

Matching Cards Controls

On desktop, use your mouse or keyboard. On phones and tablets, simply tap the cards. The board automatically resizes to fit smaller screens without requiring horizontal scrolling.

  • Mouse: Click a card to flip it.
  • Touch: Tap a card to flip it.
  • Keyboard: Use Tab to focus cards and Enter or Space to flip.
  • Restart Game: Resets the current arrangement.
  • New Game: Shuffles the cards into a fresh layout.

About the Matching Cards Game

Matching Cards is a classic memory board game built around visual recall and concentration. Each symbol appears exactly twice. The challenge is remembering where previously revealed cards are located and pairing them efficiently.

This version runs entirely in your browser with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. There is no account, download, backend, or server-side gameplay required.

Tips for a Better Score

  • Focus on card positions instead of flipping randomly.
  • When you reveal a symbol you have seen before, remember its earlier location.
  • Matching consecutive pairs builds a streak and awards a larger score bonus.
  • Fewer moves and faster completion produce a stronger final score.

Matching Cards FAQ

Is Matching Cards free to play?

Yes. This Matching Cards game is free to play directly in a modern web browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The game runs in the browser and does not require an app, plugin, or game download.

Does the game work on mobile?

Yes. The board supports touch controls and adapts to common phone and tablet screen sizes.

How is the score calculated?

You earn points for matching pairs, receive streak bonuses for consecutive matches, and lose a small number of points for incorrect pairs. Your move count and completion time remain visible so you can compare attempts.
