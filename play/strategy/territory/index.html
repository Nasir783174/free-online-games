How to Play Territory

Start from your colored territory and move into open space. Your trail marks the route you take. When you safely return to your territory, the enclosed area becomes yours.

  • Stay connected to your existing territory when you begin and finish a capture.
  • Do not cross your own exposed trail or the opponent's trail.
  • Keep an eye on the opponent. A trail left outside its territory is vulnerable.
  • Capture large, safe areas instead of taking unnecessary risks.

Territory Game Strategy

The safest approach is to make short expansions first, then use the new border as a launch point. Long trails can win more land, but they also give the opponent more opportunities to cut you off.

The match ends when the board is fully claimed or a player loses their base. The player with the larger territory wins.

About Territory Game

Is Territory free to play?

Yes. This browser version is designed to run directly on the page without downloads or an account.

Can I play Territory on mobile?

Yes. Use the on-screen directional controls. The board scales to fit smaller screens.

How is territory captured?

When you leave your area and return to it without hitting a trail, the enclosed open cells are converted to your territory.
