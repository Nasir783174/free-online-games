    How to Play Resource Management

    You have two actions in each season. Use them to construct buildings or support your community, then end the season to collect production and pay population upkeep. Every choice affects what you can afford next.

    1. Check your supplies. Food and energy are consumed at the end of every season.
    2. Choose two actions. Build production sites, trade at the market, or hold a festival.
    3. Read the forecast. Seasonal events can help or reduce production.
    4. Meet all objectives. Finish Season 12 with 20 people, 60% happiness, and 40 coins.

    About the Resource Management Game

    This free online strategy game turns resource planning into a short, replayable challenge. Farms, solar fields, workshops, and markets form a connected economy: one building supplies what another action needs, while population growth raises both your earning potential and upkeep costs.

    Each run creates a different sequence of events, so the best build order can change. The game is played entirely in your browser with no download or account.

    Resource Management Strategy Tips

    01

    Secure essentials first

    A growing population consumes more food and energy. Build enough production before pushing growth.

    02

    Watch dependencies

    Workshops create valuable materials, but their seasonal production also uses energy.

    03

    Protect happiness

    Shortages reduce happiness quickly. Festivals help, but prevention is usually more efficient.

    Resource Management Game FAQ

    Is this resource management game free?

    Yes. You can play the complete game online for free in a modern browser.

    Can I play on a mobile phone?

    Yes. The layout and controls are designed for touch screens as well as desktop browsers.

    How do I win the game?

    Complete Season 12 with at least 20 people, 60% happiness, and 40 coins. Your settlement must also avoid a total resource collapse.

    Does the game save my progress?

    Your current run is saved automatically in your browser. You can continue after refreshing the page on the same device.
