How to Play Car Racing

Control your car across three lanes and avoid the traffic coming toward you. Your score increases as you travel. Collect blue fuel canisters for bonus points and a small speed boost.

MoveUse ← / → or A / D on desktop.
MobileTap the left and right buttons to change lanes.
PausePress P or use the pause button whenever you need a break.

Car Racing Tips

  • Watch the next lane before moving so you do not switch into another car.
  • Fuel pickups are useful, but never risk a collision just to collect one.
  • The road becomes faster over time, so make smaller, earlier moves as the score grows.

About This Car Racing Game

This browser-based Car Racing game uses a simple three-lane endless-racing format. It is lightweight, works without an account or server, and saves your best score locally in your browser. The game is designed for quick desktop and mobile sessions.

Car Racing Game FAQ

Can I play Car Racing online for free?

Yes. The game runs directly in a modern web browser and does not require a download or account.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. Touch controls are provided for moving between lanes and pausing the race.

How is the score calculated?

Your score rises with distance traveled. Fuel pickups add bonus points, and longer runs increase the challenge.
