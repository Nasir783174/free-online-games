How to Play Drag Racing

Start the race, build speed, and shift up before the engine reaches the red zone. Good shifts keep your acceleration strong; late shifts cause an RPM penalty.

Controls

  • Launch: start the race and accelerate from the line.
  • Shift Up: move to the next gear at the right RPM.
  • Brake: temporarily reduce speed and RPM when you need control.
  • Restart: begin the five-race challenge again.

Drag Racing Tips

Watch the RPM bar rather than shifting randomly. Aim for the green zone, keep your gear changes smooth, and avoid holding the engine in the red zone for too long.
