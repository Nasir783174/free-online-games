How to Play Basketball

This Basketball Game is a quick shooting challenge. Your goal is to make as many successful shots as possible before the 45-second timer reaches zero.

Controls

  • Mouse: click and drag from the basketball, then release.
  • Touch: press on the basketball, drag to set power and direction, then lift your finger.
  • Keyboard: press R to restart the current game.

Basketball Game Tips

  • Use a smooth, consistent release rather than maximum power on every shot.
  • Watch the hoop position because it shifts after successful baskets.
  • Aim slightly above the rim to give the ball enough arc to drop through the basket.
  • Keep practicing the same release angle to build a reliable rhythm.

Basketball Game FAQ

Can I play this Basketball Game on a phone?

Yes. The game supports touch dragging and adapts its play area to smaller screens.

Is the Basketball Game free to play?

Yes. It runs directly in your browser with no download, account, database, or server-side game processing.

How is the score calculated?

Each successful basket adds 2 points. The timer continues while the ball is in play, so quick accurate shots help you maximize your score.
