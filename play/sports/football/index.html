How to Play Football

Choose where to place your shot, set the power, then press Shoot. Your aim marker moves across the goal and the goalkeeper reacts to each attempt.

  • Move the Aim slider to target the left, center, or right side of the goal.
  • Adjust Power for a faster, harder shot.
  • Score as many goals as possible before the 60-second game ends.
  • Misses and saves count as shots, so balance accuracy with power.

About the Football Game

This free Football Game is a quick browser penalty challenge built for desktop and mobile play. Each shot uses a target, power, and goalkeeper position to create a simple skill-based scoring system.

Football Game Tips

Mix up your target instead of repeatedly shooting to the same side. Higher power helps the ball reach the net quickly, while accurate placement gives you a better chance of avoiding the goalkeeper.

Football Game FAQ

Is the Football Game free?

Yes. The game runs directly in your browser without a download, account, database, or server-side game processing.

Can I play Football on mobile?

Yes. The sliders and large action buttons are designed for touch screens, and the game scales to smaller screens.

How is the score calculated?

A successful goal awards points based on shot power and placement accuracy. A saved or missed shot scores no points.
