Rules and controls

How to Play Pool

This is a solo 8-ball challenge. Pocket every colored and striped ball before sinking the black 8-ball. If the 8-ball goes in early, the game ends.

1

Aim the cue

Press or touch the white cue ball and drag in the opposite direction of your intended shot.

2

Set the power

Drag farther for a stronger shot. The power meter shows how hard you will strike.

3

Release to shoot

Let go to strike the cue ball. Wait for every ball to stop before taking the next shot.

Game guide

About This Online Pool Game

Pool is a cue sport built around angles, speed control, and planning ahead. This free online version turns those fundamentals into a compact single-player challenge that works directly in a desktop or mobile browser.

The table uses simulated ball-to-ball collisions, cushion rebounds, rolling friction, and six working pockets. Your score rewards accurate pocketing and multi-ball combinations, while scratches cost points and force the cue ball back onto the table.

Strategy

Tips for Better Pool Shots

  • Use moderate power. Softer shots are easier to control and reduce accidental scratches.
  • Follow the aim line. It shows the cue ball’s initial path, but collisions will change the final direction.
  • Plan for the next ball. Think about where the cue ball will stop after contact.
  • Protect the 8-ball. Leave it alone until every other numbered ball is pocketed.

Common questions

Pool Game FAQ

Can I play this Pool game for free?

Yes. The game is free to play in your browser and does not require a download, login, or account.

Does the game work on mobile?

Yes. Touch the cue ball, drag to aim and set power, then release to shoot. The table automatically fits smaller screens.

How do I win?

Pocket all numbered balls except the 8-ball, then sink the black 8-ball. Pocketing the 8-ball early ends the game.

What happens after a scratch?

A scratch deducts 75 points. Once the other balls stop, the cue ball is safely returned to the table for your next shot.
