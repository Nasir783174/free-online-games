How to Play the Volleyball Game

Your goal is to make the volleyball land on the CPU side of the court. Move left or right to get underneath the ball, then jump to bump it across the net. You can redirect a shot by contacting the ball while moving.

  1. Take position. Follow the ball and stay between it and your half of the court.
  2. Time your jump. Jump just before the ball reaches you to create a higher, stronger return.
  3. Find open space. Push forward or backward during contact to change the ball's direction.
  4. Win the match. Reach 7 points with a two-point lead before the CPU does.

About This Free Online Volleyball Game

This browser volleyball game turns the sport into a quick arcade challenge. Each side has one player, one bounce ends the rally, and every rally awards a point. The simplified rules make a match easy to start while movement, timing, and shot direction provide room to improve.

The entire game runs in your browser with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. There is no download, account, or installation required.

Tips for Winning More Rallies

  • Return toward the middle after each shot so you can cover both corners.
  • Jump into the ball instead of waiting for it to drop onto your player.
  • Move forward during contact for a flatter attack over the net.
  • Use a softer backward touch when the CPU is waiting near the net.

Common questions

Volleyball Game FAQ

Is this volleyball game free to play?

Yes. The game is free to play in a modern browser and does not require a download or account.

Can I play the volleyball game on mobile?

Yes. Use the on-screen left, right, and Jump buttons on a phone or tablet. Landscape orientation gives you the largest court view.

How do I win a match?

Be the first player to reach at least 7 points with a lead of 2 points. A rally ends when the ball touches the court.

Does the game save my score?

The current match score stays active while the page remains open. Starting a new match resets both scores.
