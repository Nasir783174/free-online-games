Quick guide

How to Play the Archery Game

Aim the crosshair at the target, then hold to draw the bow. Release when the power meter reaches the level you want. Stronger shots fly straighter, while lower-power arrows give the wind more time to move them sideways.

  1. 1
    Aim

    Move your mouse or finger to position the crosshair.

  2. 2
    Draw

    Press and hold to increase the shot power.

  3. 3
    Release

    Let go to shoot. The closer to the center, the higher the score.

Scoring the Target

10 8 6 4 2

About the game

About This Free Archery Game

This browser archery game turns target shooting into a short skill challenge. Each round gives you ten arrows, a moving target, and wind that changes after every shot. No download or account is required.

The target speeds up as the round progresses, so later arrows demand quicker aim and better power control.

Tips for a Higher Score

  • Watch the wind before you draw.
  • Aim slightly into the wind to compensate.
  • Use high power to reduce wind drift.
  • Track the target’s direction before releasing.

Common questions

Archery Game FAQ

Is this archery game free to play?

Yes. You can play the complete archery game free in a modern browser without creating an account.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Touch and drag on the game area to aim, keep holding to build power, and lift your finger to release the arrow.

How do I score a bullseye?

Land the arrow in the small gold center of the moving target. A bullseye awards 10 points.

Does the wind affect every shot?

Yes. The wind changes after each arrow and pushes lower-power shots farther sideways than high-power shots.
