How to Play Baseball

Each at-bat gives you a chance to hit the incoming pitch. The closer your swing is to the ideal contact point, the better the result. Perfect timing can produce a home run, while early or late swings can turn into singles, outs, or strikes.

  • Press Space or tap Swing to hit.
  • Watch the ball and swing as it reaches the plate.
  • Three strikes end the current game.
  • Score runs by making successful hits and keep playing through nine innings.

Baseball Scoring

Baseball Game Tips

Watch the approach

Focus on the ball as it travels toward the plate rather than reacting too early.

Wait for the zone

The best contact happens when the ball is close to the glowing hit zone.

Chase consistency

Solid singles are safer than wild swings. Protect your strikes and build your score.
