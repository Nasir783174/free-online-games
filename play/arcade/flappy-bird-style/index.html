Instructions

How to Play the Flappy Bird-Style Game

Your bird falls naturally because of gravity. Each tap, click, or key press pushes it upward. Guide it through the opening between the upper and lower pipes without touching a pipe, the ground, or the top edge.

The game becomes faster as your score rises. If you collide with an obstacle, the round ends immediately. Select Play again or New game to reset the course and try for a higher score.

⌨

Simple controls

Use Space, the Up Arrow, a mouse click, or a screen tap. The pause key is P.

◎

About the game

A Fast, Skill-Based Flying Challenge

This free online Flappy Bird-style game is a lightweight arcade challenge built for short sessions. Its one-action control is easy to understand, while narrow gaps and changing speed make high scores difficult to master.

Your best score is saved only in your current browser when local storage is available. The game runs entirely on your device and does not require an account, game download, or server connection after the page has loaded.

Flappy Bird-Style Game Tips

Watch the next gap

Focus slightly ahead of the bird so you can plan your height before reaching a pipe.

Stay near the center

A middle position gives you more room to correct upward or downward.

Tap with a rhythm

Small, consistent adjustments are usually safer than several rapid flaps.

Pause when distracted

Use P or the Pause button instead of trying to recover after looking away.

Common questions

Flappy Bird-Style Game FAQ

Is this Flappy Bird-style game free?

Yes. You can play the full game free in a modern web browser without downloading anything or creating an account.

How do I control the bird?

Press Space or the Up Arrow, click the game canvas, or tap the screen. Each input makes the bird flap upward.

How is the score calculated?

You receive one point for each complete pair of pipes the bird passes. The round ends if the bird touches a pipe or the edge of the flight area.

Does the game work on mobile?

Yes. The game board scales to fit small screens and supports tapping the board or the large Flap button.
