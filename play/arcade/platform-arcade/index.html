Quick guide

How to Play Platform Arcade

Guide Nova through each stage and collect all glowing energy cores. The exit portal unlocks only after every core in the level has been collected.

  • Move: Use A and D, the left and right arrow keys, or the on-screen buttons.
  • Jump: Press W, the up arrow, Space, or the Jump button. Hold briefly for a higher jump.
  • Pause: Press P or use the Pause button below the game.
  • Survive: Avoid spikes and patrol bots. Falling off a stage costs one life.

Player tips

Score More Points

  • Collect cores quickly for a larger time bonus.
  • Landing on a patrol bot disables it and earns points.
  • Use moving platforms to reach hidden routes.
  • Activate checkpoints before risky jumps.

Inside the game

About Platform Arcade

Platform Arcade is a compact free online platform game built around responsive movement and short, replayable stages. Each level introduces a new challenge, including patrol enemies, disappearing routes, moving platforms, and carefully placed hazards.

The game runs entirely in your browser. Your best score is saved on your device, so you can return and try to beat it without creating an account.

Questions

Platform Arcade FAQ

Is Platform Arcade free to play?

Yes. You can play the complete game free in your browser without downloading anything or creating an account.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Use the large left, right, and Jump controls below the game. The game area scales to fit smaller screens.

How do I unlock the exit portal?

Collect every energy core in the current stage. The portal changes from red to green when it is ready.

Does the game save my score?

Your best score is stored locally in your browser. No account or online storage is required.
