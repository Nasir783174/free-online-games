Snake Game

Eat the food and grow your snake. Don't hit the walls or yourself.

Controls: Use Arrow Keys or WASD. On mobile, swipe the board or use the buttons. Press Space to pause.

How to Play Snake

Start the game and control the snake as it moves around the board. Guide the snake toward the food to increase your score and make the snake longer. The challenge becomes harder as your snake grows and its movement speed increases.

01

Move the snake

Use the arrow keys or WASD on desktop. On mobile, swipe in the direction you want the snake to travel.

02

Eat the food

Move the snake over the food to collect it. Every food item increases your score and adds another segment to the snake.

03

Avoid collisions

Don't run into the edge of the game board or into the snake's own body. Either collision ends the game.

Snake Game Tips and Strategy

  • Keep enough open space around the snake instead of making sharp turns unnecessarily.
  • Plan your route before collecting food when the snake becomes longer.
  • Avoid trapping yourself in a corner or creating a closed loop you cannot escape.
  • Use the edges carefully because hitting the wall immediately ends the game.
  • Focus on surviving as well as collecting food. A longer run can produce a better score.

About Snake Game

Snake is a classic arcade-style game built around simple movement and increasingly difficult decision-making. The snake continuously travels across a grid while the player changes its direction to collect food. Each successful pickup makes the snake longer, reducing the available space and making future movements more challenging.

This browser version is designed for quick play without downloads or accounts. Your current high score is stored locally in your browser, so the game remains completely client-side.

Snake Game FAQ

Can I play Snake Game for free?

Yes. This Snake Game is designed for free browser gameplay and does not require a download, login, or account.

Can I play Snake on a mobile phone?

Yes. You can swipe across the game board or use the on-screen direction buttons on supported mobile devices.

How is the Snake score calculated?

Each food item gives you 10 points. Your score increases as you successfully collect more food.

What happens when the snake hits itself?

The game ends when the snake's head collides with its body. You can then restart and try to beat your previous high score.

Does Snake require a download?

No. The game runs directly in a modern web browser using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
