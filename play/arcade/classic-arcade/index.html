How to Play Classic Arcade

Your ship starts at the bottom of the arena. Clear every enemy before the fleet crosses the red defense line. Each completed formation starts a faster wave with more pressure.

  1. 1
    Move into position

    Slide left or right to line up a safe shot and avoid enemy fire.

  2. 2
    Break the formation

    Fire at the descending ships. Different enemy rows award different points.

  3. 3
    Protect your lives

    A direct hit costs one life and gives your ship a brief moment of protection.

About the Classic Arcade Game

Classic Arcade is an original browser shooter inspired by the simple, skill-based games found in early arcades. The rules are easy to understand, but later waves demand quicker movement, smarter positioning, and accurate timing. Nothing needs to be installed, and your best score is stored only in your current browser.

Classic Arcade FAQ

Is Classic Arcade free to play?

Yes. The game is free to play directly in a modern browser and does not require a download or account.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Use the on-screen left, fire, and right controls. The game area scales to fit smaller screens.

How does scoring work?

Enemy ships award 10, 20, or 30 points depending on their row. Clearing a wave awards a 100-point bonus, and later waves move faster.

Is my high score saved?

Your high score is saved in local browser storage on the device you use. It is not sent to a server.
