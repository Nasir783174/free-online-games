    Select the reaction area to begin. The screen will enter a waiting state for a random amount of time. Do not react yet.

    About the Reaction Game

    This free online Reaction Game measures the delay between a visual signal and your response. Each round uses a different waiting time, so you cannot rely on a fixed countdown. Your final result is based on five successful attempts.

    Reaction speed can change with focus, device input, display refresh rate, fatigue, and practice. Treat the score as a casual challenge rather than a medical or scientific assessment. Your personal best is saved only in your current browser.

    Reaction Game FAQ

    What is a good reaction time?

    For this casual browser game, a result around 200–300 milliseconds is typically quick. Results vary by device, browser, attention, and input method.

    Can I play the Reaction Game on mobile?

    Yes. Tap the large reaction area to start each test and tap it again as soon as the signal turns green.

    Why does my reaction score change?

    Natural variation is normal. Sleep, focus, anticipation, screen refresh rate, and the delay added by a mouse or touchscreen can all affect a result.

    Does the game save my score?

    The fastest valid reaction is stored in your browser as a personal best. No account, database, or server is used.

    Is this a scientific reaction-time test?

    No. It is a casual game designed for entertainment and informal comparison, not medical or scientific measurement.
