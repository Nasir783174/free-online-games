How to Play Time Management

Time Management is a casual strategy game about making quick decisions during a simulated workday. Read each task, judge its value and urgency, and spend your limited time carefully.

Controls

Click or tap a task card to complete it. Use Pause to stop the timer and Restart Day to begin the current day again.

Tips for Better Scores

Prioritize urgent tasks when they appear, avoid wasting time on low-value work, and keep your focus high. Later days become faster and more demanding.

About the Game

This free browser game is designed for short casual sessions. No account or download is required, and your progress is kept only in the current browser session.
