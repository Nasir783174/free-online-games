How to Play Relaxing Game

Start the session and guide the small orb around the garden. Move toward the floating lights to collect them. Each light adds to your score and a new one appears somewhere else.

  • Move slowly with the keyboard or touch controls.
  • Collect glowing lights to build your score.
  • Stay inside the garden and avoid rushing.
  • The 60-second session ends automatically.

A simple way to unwind

This free Relaxing Game is designed as a low-pressure casual browser experience. There are no lives to lose, difficult levels, or complicated objectives—just a small space to pause and focus.

Tips

Try following the on-screen breathing cue while you move. Short, unhurried movements make the session feel more natural.
