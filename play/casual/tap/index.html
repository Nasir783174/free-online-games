Quick guide

How to Play Tap

Start the game and select each blue target before it moves. Fast, accurate hits build your combo and increase the points earned from every normal target. Gold targets are rare and award a larger bonus.

Do not select red decoys. A decoy costs points and breaks your combo. The round ends when the 30-second timer reaches zero.

About the game

About Tap

Tap is a short casual game built around reaction speed, visual attention, and accuracy. Targets move faster as the round continues, so the final seconds demand quicker decisions than the opening moments.

The game runs directly in your browser with no download or account. Your best score is saved only on your device.

Improve your score

Tap Game Tips

  • Watch the center of the arena so your eyes travel less.
  • Prioritize accuracy; a broken combo costs more than a slow hit.
  • Pause if you are interrupted instead of sacrificing your streak.
  • On mobile, use the hand that comfortably reaches the whole arena.

Common questions

Tap Game FAQ

Is the Tap game free to play?

Yes. Tap is free to play in a modern browser and requires no download, signup, or purchase.

Can I play Tap on a phone?

Yes. The arena and targets resize for phones and tablets, and the game supports touch input.

How is the Tap score calculated?

Normal targets award 10 points plus a combo bonus. Gold targets award 50 points plus a combo bonus. Red decoys remove 25 points and reset the combo.

Does the game save my score?

The best score is stored in your browser on your current device. No score is sent to a server.
