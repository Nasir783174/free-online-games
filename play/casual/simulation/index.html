How to Play Simulation

Simulation is a casual town-management game where each turn represents one day. Choose one action, then end the day to move time forward. Your goal is to grow the population while keeping enough money, energy, services, and happiness to support the town.

Build strategically

Homes increase residents, while jobs help your economy keep pace with growth.

Watch resources

Energy and services affect daily happiness and the town's ability to grow.

Handle events

Random events can help or hurt your budget, population, or community mood.

About the Simulation Game

This browser-based Simulation game is designed around simple management decisions rather than complicated controls. Every day gives you a meaningful choice, and the town changes based on your decisions. There is no download or account required, so you can replay and try a different strategy whenever a game ends.

Simulation Tips

  • Do not expand housing too quickly if your job supply is low.
  • Use parks when happiness starts falling instead of waiting for a crisis.
  • Improve power and services before the town becomes heavily populated.
  • Keep a cash reserve so a bad event does not immediately end your run.

FAQ

Is Simulation free to play?

Yes. This Simulation game runs directly in your browser and does not require a download, login, or account.

How do I control the game?

Choose an action with your mouse, keyboard focus, or touch, then select End Day. No keyboard-only gameplay is required.

How does the score work?

Your town score is based on population, happiness, available resources, and the number of days you survive.
