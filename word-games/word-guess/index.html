How to Play Word Guess

Enter a five-letter word and press Enter. A green tile means the letter is correct and in the correct position. A yellow tile means the letter is in the hidden word but belongs in another position. A gray tile means the letter is not in the word.

Controls

Use your keyboard on desktop or the on-screen keyboard on phones and tablets. Backspace removes the last letter, and Enter submits your guess.

✔ Free to play✔ No download✔ Works on mobile

About Word Guess

Word Guess is a browser-based word puzzle built for quick rounds. There is no download, account, or server connection required. Start a new round whenever you want and try to improve your score.

Word Guess Tips

  • Start with a word containing several common vowels and consonants.
  • Use yellow letters in new positions rather than repeating them in the same place.
  • Use the remaining attempts to narrow down the word instead of guessing randomly.

Word Guess FAQ

How many guesses do I get?

You get six guesses for each five-letter word.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. The game includes a large on-screen keyboard designed for touch devices.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Word Guess runs directly in a modern web browser.
