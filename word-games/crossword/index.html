How to Play Crossword

Choose a square in the crossword grid and solve the matching clue. Enter one letter at a time. The active clue and its answer cells are highlighted so you can see where the word belongs.

  • Select a clue or grid square to choose a word.
  • Type letters with your keyboard, or use the on-screen controls on mobile.
  • Press Space to switch between Across and Down when a square belongs to both.
  • Use Backspace or Erase to remove a letter.
  • Complete every answer correctly to finish the puzzle.

About Crossword Games

A crossword is a word puzzle built around intersecting Across and Down answers. Each clue gives a definition, description, or wordplay prompt, while shared letters help confirm answers. This browser crossword uses a compact grid designed for a quick, approachable puzzle session.

Crossword Tips

  • Start with clues whose answers you know immediately.
  • Use crossing letters to narrow down uncertain answers.
  • Short answers can be useful anchors for the rest of the grid.
  • If you get stuck, move to another clue and return after adding more crossing letters.

Crossword FAQ

Can I play this Crossword Game for free?

Yes. The puzzle runs directly in your browser with no account or download required.

Can I play Crossword on a phone?

Yes. The responsive grid and touch-friendly controls adapt to smaller screens.

How does scoring work?

You earn points for correctly completed letters, with a completion bonus when the whole crossword is solved. Incorrect letters do not add points.
