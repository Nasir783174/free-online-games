Word Search

Play Word Search free online in your browser. Find hidden words in a responsive puzzle with keyboard, mouse, and touch controls. No download required.

How to Play Word Search

Look through the grid for each word in the list. Words can run horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, and they may appear forwards or backwards.

  1. Press or click the first letter of a word.
  2. Drag or move to the final letter in a straight line.
  3. Release to check your selection.
  4. Find all 12 words to complete the puzzle.

Word Search Controls

  • Mouse: click and drag across a word.
  • Touch: press and drag across the letters.
  • Keyboard: use arrow keys to move the active cell and Enter/Space to begin or finish a selection.
  • New Game: starts a fresh puzzle with a new word placement.
✔ Free to play✔ No download✔ Works on mobile

About Word Search

Word Search is a classic word puzzle built around pattern recognition, spelling, and careful scanning. This browser version creates a fresh letter arrangement each time you start a new game, while keeping the word list readable and the controls comfortable on both desktop and mobile screens.

Each discovered word is highlighted on the board and marked in the list. Complete the entire puzzle to see your final score and time.

Word Search Tips

Start with unusual letters or longer words, then scan in straight lines. If a word seems difficult, check diagonal lines and remember that hidden words can be reversed.

Word Search FAQ

Can I play Word Search on my phone?

Yes. The puzzle supports touch dragging and adapts its grid to smaller screens.

Are words always placed in straight lines?

Yes. Each hidden word is placed horizontally, vertically, or diagonally in a straight line.

Does the game need a download?

No. The game runs in a modern browser using client-side HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
