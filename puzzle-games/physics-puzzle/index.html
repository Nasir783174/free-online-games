How to Play Physics Puzzle

Your objective is simple: get the blue ball into the green goal. The challenge is deciding where to draw platforms so gravity and momentum carry the ball safely through each level.

  1. Draw one or more lines inside the game board.
  2. Use the ink meter carefully. Longer lines consume more ink.
  3. Select Release Ball when your setup is ready.
  4. Reach the green goal without falling off the board or touching red hazards.

You can reset a level at any time. On phones and tablets, draw with your finger directly on the board.

Physics Puzzle Tips

  • Use slopes. A small angle can create useful horizontal momentum.
  • Draw less. Efficient solutions earn a better star rating.
  • Watch the bounce. The ball keeps momentum when it hits a platform.
  • Bridge gaps. Connect safe surfaces instead of trying to catch the ball at the last second.

About Physics Puzzle

Physics Puzzle is a free online drawing and gravity game built for quick browser play. Each level combines obstacles, gaps, moving space, and limited drawing ink. There is no download or account required, and the game supports both desktop and mobile controls.

Physics Puzzle FAQ

Is Physics Puzzle free to play?

Yes. This version runs entirely in your browser and does not require a download, account, or payment.

How do I control the ball?

You do not steer the ball directly. Draw platforms first, then release it and use gravity, slopes, and rebounds to guide it toward the goal.

Does the game work on mobile?

Yes. The board scales to the available screen width and supports touch drawing on modern mobile browsers.
