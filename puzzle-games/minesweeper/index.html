Learn the rules

How to Play Minesweeper

Your goal is to reveal every square that does not contain a mine. The first square you open is always safe. After that, each revealed number tells you how many mines touch that square, including diagonally.

  1. 1 Reveal a square Click or tap any covered square. Empty areas open automatically when no neighboring mines are present.
  2. 2 Read the clues A number from 1 to 8 shows the exact number of mines in the eight surrounding positions.
  3. 3 Flag likely mines Right-click on desktop, press and hold on touch screens, or select Flag mode before tapping.
  4. 4 Clear the safe squares You win when every non-mine square is open. A flag is useful, but only safe-square clearance determines the win.

About the Minesweeper Game

Minesweeper is a deduction game built around a hidden grid. It became widely known as a computer puzzle, but its appeal comes from a simple idea: every move should follow from the clues already on the board. This browser version keeps the familiar rules while adding responsive controls for desktop and mobile play.

Minesweeper Tips and Strategy

  • Start around open space. Large empty areas expose more clues and give you useful information quickly.
  • Look for completed clues. If a number already touches that many flags, its other covered neighbors are safe.
  • Mark certain mines first. If a clue has the same number as its remaining covered neighbors, those neighbors must be mines.
  • Use chord reveal carefully. Click an open number after placing the correct number of adjacent flags to reveal its other neighbors at once.

Common questions

Minesweeper FAQ

Can I play Minesweeper for free?

Yes. This Minesweeper game is free to play in your browser and does not require a download, account, or signup.

What do the numbers mean in Minesweeper?

Each number shows how many mines are touching that square horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Use nearby clues together to identify safe squares and mines.

Is the first click always safe?

Yes. Mines are placed after your first move, keeping the selected square safe. The surrounding squares are also protected whenever the selected board allows it.

How do I place a flag on mobile?

Select Flag mode and tap a covered square. You can also press and hold a covered square. Switch back to Reveal mode when you want to open squares.
