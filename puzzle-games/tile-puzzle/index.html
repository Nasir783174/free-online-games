How to Play Tile Puzzle

The board contains numbered tiles and one empty space. Your goal is to arrange the numbers from smallest to largest, reading left to right and top to bottom, with the empty space in the final position.

  1. Select a 3 × 3, 4 × 4, or 5 × 5 puzzle.
  2. Click or tap a numbered tile directly beside the empty space.
  3. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to slide tiles.
  4. Continue until every tile is in numerical order.

Tile Puzzle Tips

Start by solving the top row, then work downward while protecting tiles already in position. Avoid moving solved sections unless it creates a clear path for the remaining numbers.

The 3 × 3 board is a good warm-up. The classic 4 × 4 version requires more planning, while 5 × 5 creates a longer challenge.

About the Tile Puzzle Game

Tile Puzzle is a browser-based sliding puzzle inspired by classic numbered sliding games. This version runs entirely on your device, needs no account or download, and generates only solvable boards. Your best move count for each board size is stored locally in your browser.

Tile Puzzle FAQ

Is this Tile Puzzle free to play?

Yes. The game is free to play directly in a modern web browser and does not require a download or account.

Can every shuffled puzzle be solved?

Yes. The game shuffles the board by making valid moves from a solved position, so each generated puzzle remains solvable.

Does Tile Puzzle work on mobile?

Yes. Tap a tile next to the empty space or swipe across the puzzle board on a touch device.
