Block Puzzle

Play Block Puzzle free online in your browser. Place block shapes, clear rows and columns, build combos, and chase a high score with no download.

How to Play Block Puzzle

  1. Choose one of the three block shapes below the board.
  2. Place the complete shape inside empty board cells.
  3. Fill an entire horizontal row or vertical column to clear it.
  4. Use all three shapes to receive a fresh set.
  5. The game ends when none of the available shapes can fit.

Block Puzzle Strategy

Keep the center flexible and avoid creating isolated single-cell gaps. Large shapes become difficult to place when the board is fragmented, so preserve open areas instead of filling every convenient corner.

Clearing more than one line in a move earns a stronger line bonus. Consecutive clearing moves also increase your combo.

About Block Puzzle

Block Puzzle is a grid-based spatial puzzle game built around shape placement and board management. Unlike falling-block games, there is no timer forcing each move. You can inspect the available pieces, plan where they fit, and decide which placement leaves the strongest board for later turns.

Block Puzzle FAQ

Is this Block Puzzle game free?

Yes. The game runs directly in your browser and does not require a download or account.

How do I score more points?

You score for every block placed and receive bonuses for completed rows and columns. Multi-line clears and consecutive clearing moves increase the reward.

Does the game work on mobile?

Yes. Tap a shape to select it, then tap a valid highlighted location on the board. The layout adapts to smaller screens.
