How to Play Sorting Puzzle

The goal is to organize the balls so every non-empty tube contains only one color. Each tube can hold four balls.

  1. Select a tube. Tap or click a tube that contains at least one ball.
  2. Choose a destination. Move the top ball into an empty tube or onto a ball of the same color.
  3. Finish every color. The level is complete when all used tubes contain four matching balls.

On desktop, you can also use the arrow keys to move focus between tubes, Enter or Space to select, U to undo, and R to restart.

About Sorting Puzzle

Sorting Puzzle is a logic game built around planning and temporary space. A move that looks useful immediately can block another color, so successful play often depends on keeping at least one flexible tube available.

Sorting Puzzle Tips

  • Keep an empty tube available when possible.
  • Build completed color groups from the bottom upward.
  • Avoid covering a color you will need on your next move.
  • Use Undo when a move creates an obvious dead end.

Sorting Puzzle FAQ

Is Sorting Puzzle free to play?

Yes. This version runs directly in the browser and does not require a download or account.

Can I play Sorting Puzzle on mobile?

Yes. The game uses large tap targets and a responsive board designed for phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

When is a level complete?

A level is complete when every non-empty tube is full and all four balls inside each tube are the same color.
