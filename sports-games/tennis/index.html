How to Play Tennis

Move your racket along the baseline and time your shots as the ball comes toward you. During a serve, press Space or HIT to send the ball into play. A return is automatically made when the ball reaches your racket at the right position.

Controls

  • Left / A: Move your racket left.
  • Right / D: Move your racket right.
  • Space / HIT: Serve and hit the ball.
  • On mobile, use the large touch buttons below the court.

About This Tennis Game

This free online tennis game uses a simple arcade-style scoring system inspired by tennis. Win points by returning the ball successfully while keeping your racket in the ball's path. The computer opponent adjusts its position and shot direction automatically.

Tips for Winning

  • Watch the ball rather than the opponent's racket.
  • Stay near the center until you see the return direction.
  • Use small movements to line up your racket for more consistent returns.
  • On mobile, tap HIT as the ball reaches your side.
