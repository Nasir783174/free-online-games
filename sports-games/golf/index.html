Golf

Play Golf Game free online in your browser. Aim, adjust power, and complete 9 holes with as few strokes as possible. No download required.

How to Play Golf

Each hole gives you a target and a ball position. Set your aim toward the cup, select enough power to reach it, then take your shot. The course includes sand and trees that can affect your ball.

Controls

  • Adjust Aim to choose the shot direction.
  • Adjust Power to control how far the ball travels.
  • Select Take Shot to hit the ball.
  • Use Restart Game any time to start a fresh 9-hole round.

About This Golf Game

This browser golf game uses a simple stroke-based scoring system. Your goal is to complete 9 holes with the lowest total score. Each hole has a par value, and your result is shown as strokes relative to par.

Scoring

  • Eagle: 2 under par
  • Birdie: 1 under par
  • Par: equal to par
  • Bogey: 1 over par

Golf Tips & Strategy

01 Aim for the center of the cup rather than its edge.

02 Use moderate power when the hole is short to avoid overshooting.

03 On longer holes, favor controlled shots over maximum power.

04 Plan around obstacles instead of repeatedly hitting into them.

Golf Game FAQ

Can I play this Golf Game for free?

Yes. The game runs directly in your browser and does not require a download or account.

How many holes are in the game?

The round contains 9 holes with different layouts and par values.

Can I play Golf on mobile?

Yes. The aim and power controls are touch-friendly, and the game is designed to fit smaller screens.
