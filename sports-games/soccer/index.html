Soccer

Play a fast, free Soccer Game online in your browser. Shoot past the goalkeeper, score goals, and beat your high score with no download required.

How to Play Soccer Game

Your objective is simple: score goals by shooting the ball past the goalkeeper. Aim inside the goal, set a comfortable shot power, and press Shoot. You have 10 shots in each game.

  • Move the pointer or finger across the field to aim.
  • Use the Shot Power slider to change the strength of your kick.
  • Click, tap, or press Space to shoot.
  • Score as many goals as possible before all 10 shots are used.

Soccer Game Tips

  • Aim toward the corners to make saves harder.
  • Moderate power gives you more control than maximum power.
  • Watch the goalkeeper's position before every shot.
  • Use the full width of the goal instead of repeating the same target.

About This Soccer Game

This browser soccer game is a lightweight, client-side sports game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It requires no account, download, database, or server-side game processing. Your score is kept in the current game session.

Soccer Game FAQ

Can I play this Soccer Game on mobile?

Yes. The field supports touch aiming and touch shooting, with a responsive layout for smaller screens.

How many shots do I get?

Each round gives you 10 shots. Your final score is the number of goals you score.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The game is designed to run directly in a modern browser.
