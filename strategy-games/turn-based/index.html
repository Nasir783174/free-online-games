Battle guide

How to Play Turn-Based

Each combatant acts once per round. On your turn, select an action and wait for the Iron Warden to respond. Reduce the Warden’s health to zero before your own health runs out.

Read the arena

Check both health bars, energy pips, and active guard effects before choosing a move.

Manage energy

Strike costs nothing. Guard reduces the next hit and restores one energy. Stronger actions cost two energy.

Time your recovery

Recover restores health but has a two-turn cooldown. Use it before the enemy can finish the battle.

Strategy tip

Guarding is strongest when the Warden has two or more energy. It can soften a Power Strike while preparing your next attack.

About the game

A Quick Tactical Battle

Turn-Based is a free online strategy game built around deliberate choices rather than fast reactions. Every action changes the next decision: a basic attack keeps pressure on the opponent, a guard prepares energy, a Power Strike creates a large health swing, and recovery can extend the fight.

The computer opponent considers health, available energy, active defenses, and recovery cooldowns. Battles are short enough for a quick break but varied enough to reward a different plan each time.

Questions

Turn-Based Game FAQ

Is this turn-based game free to play?

Yes. The complete game is free to play in your browser and does not require a download, login, or account.

Does the game work on mobile?

Yes. The battle arena and action controls adapt to smaller screens, and every move can be selected by touch.

How is the score calculated?

You earn points for dealing damage, blocking attacks, and restoring health. Winning adds a victory bonus based partly on your remaining health.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Once the page files are available locally, the game logic runs entirely in the browser. The optional web font is the only external page asset.
