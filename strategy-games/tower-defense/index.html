How to Play Tower Defense

Select a tower, then click or tap an open grass tile beside the road. Start each wave when your defenses are ready. Enemies that reach the crystal cost one life, so strengthen weak sections before the next group arrives.

  1. Build Archer towers for reliable early coverage.
  2. Add Cannons where groups stay close together.
  3. Use Frost towers to slow enemies inside other towers’ range.
  4. Select a built tower to upgrade or sell it.

About This Tower Defense Game

Tower Defense is a browser strategy game about placement, timing, and resource management. Each defeated enemy earns coins and score. Later waves bring faster, tougher opponents, so a few unplanned towers will not be enough.

This version has ten handcrafted waves, three complementary tower classes, upgrades, selling, two game speeds, and touch controls. Everything runs locally in your browser with no account or download.

Tower Defense Game FAQ

Is this Tower Defense game free?

Yes. You can play every wave for free in a modern web browser without creating an account.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Select towers and tap the battlefield to build. The layout and controls adapt to smaller screens.

How do I win?

Keep at least one life after defeating all enemies in wave ten. Towers can be upgraded to handle the tougher final waves.

What does each tower do?

Archers provide balanced single-target damage, Cannons deal splash damage, and Frost towers slow enemies while attacking.
