War Strategy

Play War Strategy free online in your browser. Command your army, capture territory, manage resources, and defeat the enemy without downloads.

How to Play War Strategy

Choose one of your territories, then attack an adjacent enemy or neutral territory. Successful attacks expand your control, while fortifying increases a territory's defense.

1. Build your position

Use fortify orders when a border territory needs stronger defense.

2. Choose attacks carefully

Attacking costs army units, so avoid spreading your forces too thin.

3. Manage resources

Gold and supplies are restored during your turn, helping you sustain your campaign.

About War Strategy

This free online strategy game is designed around territory control, resource management, tactical attacks, and turn-based decisions. The goal is to eliminate the opposing force before your turns run out.

Tips & Strategy

  • Secure the center before making a deep push into enemy territory.
  • Keep enough army strength behind your frontline to protect captured regions.
  • Fortify valuable border regions before ending a turn.
  • Neutral regions are easier targets and can provide useful strategic routes.
