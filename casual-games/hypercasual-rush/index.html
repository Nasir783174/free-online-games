How to Play Hypercasual Rush

Your runner moves forward automatically across three lanes. Move left or right before a red barrier reaches you. Collect gold stars whenever it is safe—their value increases when you keep a clean collection streak.

  1. Start the run. Select Start Game and watch the road ahead.
  2. Change lanes. Use Left and Right to avoid every barrier.
  3. Collect stars. Each star adds points and improves your streak multiplier.
  4. Stay sharp. The road accelerates as your score rises.

About Hypercasual Rush

Hypercasual Rush is a lightweight endless runner designed around immediate controls and quick replay. Each attempt starts gently, then introduces faster object movement and tighter decisions. Your score grows with distance, while stars reward precise lane changes and controlled risk.

The game runs entirely in your browser with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. Nothing needs to be installed, and your best score is stored only in your current browser.
