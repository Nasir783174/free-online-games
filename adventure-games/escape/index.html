How to Play Escape

Explore the Clockmaker’s Study one view at a time. Select objects that look useful, inspect anything unusual, and use the clues you discover to open locked areas. Collected objects move into your inventory automatically.

Some objects work together. Select one inventory item and then another to combine them. Escape through the study door before the ten-minute timer reaches zero.

About the game

What Is the Escape Game?

Escape is a compact point-and-click adventure inspired by classic room-escape puzzles. The entire game runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no account is needed. Progress depends on observation and deduction rather than fast reactions.

Strategy

Tips for Escaping the Study

  • Check every room view before spending a hint.
  • Record the order of symbols as well as their values.
  • Revisit dark or locked areas after finding an item.
  • Try combining inventory items that seem incomplete.

Quick answers

Escape Game FAQ

Is this Escape game free to play?

Yes. The complete room-escape adventure is free to play in a modern web browser.

Do I need to download the game?

No. Escape runs with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript directly in your browser.

Can I play Escape on a phone?

Yes. Every interactive object and inventory item supports touch controls, and the layout adapts to smaller screens.

How long does one game take?

You have ten minutes per attempt. Most successful runs take about three to eight minutes.
