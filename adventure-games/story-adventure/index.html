How to Play Story

Read each scene and choose the action that best fits your plan. Your choices change your Courage, Supplies, clues, route, score, and final ending.

  1. Read the scene. Important details may hint at safer or more rewarding choices.
  2. Choose an action. Tap a choice or use number keys 1–3 on a keyboard.
  3. Manage resources. Courage helps with dangerous actions. Supplies help solve practical problems.
  4. Find an ending. Your route and discoveries decide how Ember Hollow’s story concludes.

Adventure Tips

  • Gathering information can unlock stronger choices later.
  • A bold route may score more, but it can cost Courage.
  • Not every peaceful choice is safe, and not every risk is reckless.
  • Replay to discover all six endings.

About the game

What Is the Story Adventure Game?

Story is a free browser-based choice adventure. Instead of reflex controls, you shape the journey through decisions. Each playthrough branches through Ember Hollow’s forest, river, ruins, or mountain pass, with different clues and outcomes to uncover.

Questions

Story Game FAQ

Is Story free to play online?

Yes. Story is free to play in a modern browser, with no download, account, or installation required.

Does the game have multiple endings?

Yes. There are six endings. Your route, resources, clues, and final decisions determine which one you reach.

Can I play Story on mobile?

Yes. The layout and choice controls adapt to phones and tablets. Tap a choice to continue the adventure.

Does Story save personal information?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser and does not require an account or send gameplay data to a server.
