Maze Adventure

Play Maze Adventure free online. Explore changing ruins, collect lost relics, avoid guardians, and escape three browser-based maze levels with no download.

How to Play Maze Adventure

Guide the explorer through the corridors and collect every relic in the current maze. The exit stays sealed until the last relic is recovered. Reach the open exit to advance, and clear all three levels to win the expedition.

Controls

  • Desktop: use the arrow keys or W, A, S, and D.
  • Mobile: swipe across the maze or tap the direction buttons.
  • Restart: select Restart Run to generate a fresh maze from level one.

About Maze Adventure

Maze Adventure combines a classic maze puzzle with light exploration and survival mechanics. Every free online game begins with a newly generated route. Relic locations, hazards, potions, and guardians also change, making observation as important as speed.

Maze Adventure Tips

Read the corridors

01 Read the corridors Check branching paths before committing. A short detour may hold a relic or potion.

Track guardians

02 Track guardians Guardians move after you do. Keep walls between you and them whenever possible.

Save your health

03 Save your health Potions cannot raise health above three, so leave one untouched when you are already full.

Maze Adventure FAQ

Is Maze Adventure free to play?

Yes. Maze Adventure is a free browser game with no download, login, or signup required.

Does the maze change each time?

Yes. A new maze and new item positions are generated whenever you restart the run.

Can I play Maze Adventure on mobile?

Yes. On phones and tablets, move using swipe gestures or the large on-screen direction controls.

How do I unlock the exit?

Collect every blue relic in the maze. The exit turns gold and opens after you recover the final relic.
