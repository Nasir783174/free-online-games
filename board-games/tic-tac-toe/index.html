How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Choose an empty square to place your mark. You play as X against the computer, while two-player mode lets X and O take turns on the same device.

  1. Select Vs computer or Two players.
  2. Place your mark in any open square.
  3. Complete a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of three marks to win.

The round ends immediately when someone wins or all nine squares are filled. Select New round to play again; the starting player alternates each round.

Tic Tac Toe Tips

  • 1 Control the center The center connects to four possible winning lines.
  • 2 Watch every threat Block two marks in a row before making your own plan.
  • 3 Create a fork Build two winning threats that cannot both be blocked.

About Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is a two-player strategy game played on a 3×3 grid. Players take turns placing X and O, and the first player to connect three matching marks wins. Its simple rules make it quick to learn, while careful play rewards planning and pattern recognition.

This browser version needs no download. Scores are kept on your device, so you can play several rounds against the computer or a friend.

Tic Tac Toe FAQ

Can I play Tic Tac Toe for free?

Yes. This Tic Tac Toe game is free to play in your browser and requires no download, login, or signup.

Can I play against another person?

Yes. Choose Two players to let X and O take turns on the same phone, tablet, or computer.

How does Hard mode work?

Hard mode evaluates possible moves and plays optimal Tic Tac Toe strategy. You can draw with perfect play, but you cannot force a win.

Does the game work on mobile?

Yes. Tap any open square to play. The board and controls resize for modern mobile screens.
