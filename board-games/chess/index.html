Chess

Play a free chess game online in your browser. Enjoy complete two-player chess with legal moves, checkmate detection, undo, and no download.

How to Play Chess

White moves first, then players alternate turns. Select one of your pieces to see its legal destinations. Capture an opposing piece by moving onto its square. The goal is to attack the opposing king so it has no legal escape—a position called checkmate.

Chess controls

  • Mouse or touch: Select a piece and then a highlighted destination.
  • Keyboard: Use arrow keys to focus a square and Enter or Space to select it.
  • Undo: Return to the position before the most recent move.
  • New game: Reset the board and start again with White.

Chess Rules Included

This browser chess game validates movement for every piece and prevents moves that leave your own king in check. It also supports the special rules used in standard chess.

  • ✓ Check, checkmate, and stalemate
  • ✓ Kingside and queenside castling
  • ✓ En passant captures
  • ✓ Pawn promotion choices
  • ✓ Draws by repetition, 50-move rule, or insufficient material

About This Online Chess Game

Chess is a strategy board game played on 64 squares. Each side begins with 16 pieces: one king, one queen, two rooks, two bishops, two knights, and eight pawns. Different movement patterns make development, king safety, tactics, and planning important from the opening through the endgame.

Quick strategy tips

Control the center

Central squares give your pieces more useful options.

Develop early

Bring knights and bishops into play before moving one piece repeatedly.

Protect your king

Castle when it is safe and avoid weakening the pawns around your king.

Chess Game FAQ

Is this chess game free to play?

Yes. You can play the complete chess game for free in a modern browser without downloading an app or creating an account.

Can I play chess on a phone?

Yes. The board adapts to smaller screens, and every move can be made with touch controls.

Does the game enforce legal chess moves?

Yes. It validates standard piece movement, check, castling, en passant, promotion, checkmate, stalemate, and common draw conditions.

Can I play against the computer?

This version is designed for two people playing locally on the same device. It does not use a server or computer opponent.
