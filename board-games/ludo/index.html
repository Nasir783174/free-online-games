Ludo

Play Ludo online for free in your browser. Enjoy a fast, mobile-friendly Ludo board game with local multiplayer, dice rolls, captures, safe squares, and no download required.

How to Play Ludo

Ludo is a race game for up to four local players. Each player controls four tokens. A token can leave its yard only after rolling a six. Once on the track, tokens move clockwise according to the dice value.

  1. Press Roll Dice.
  2. If you have legal moves, selectable tokens will pulse.
  3. Tap or click the token you want to move.
  4. Roll a six or make a capture to earn another roll.
  5. Move all four tokens through your colored home lane and into the center finish.

Ludo Controls

On desktop, use your mouse or keyboard. On mobile, tap the dice button and then tap a highlighted token. The board automatically scales to fit narrow screens.

  • Roll Dice: click or tap the dice / Roll Dice button.
  • Move Token: click or tap a highlighted token.
  • Keyboard: press Space or Enter to roll when available.
  • Restart: use New Game at any time.

About the Ludo Game

Ludo is a classic board game built around simple dice movement, tactical captures, safe spaces, and a final race into the home lane. This browser version keeps the familiar four-color board while using streamlined turn handling so it works well on both desktop and mobile without downloads, accounts, or server-side processing.

Ludo Tips and Strategy

  • Get more than one token onto the board so you are not dependent on a single piece.
  • Use safe squares when an opponent is close behind you.
  • Captures are valuable because they send a rival token back to its yard and award an extra roll.
  • Avoid bunching all four tokens too far from home at the same time.
  • Plan exact rolls for the home lane; overshooting is not allowed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play this Ludo game for free?

Yes. The game runs directly in your browser and does not require a download, account, or payment.

Does this Ludo game work on mobile?

Yes. The board is responsive and the game supports touch controls for dice rolls and token movement.

How do I get a token out of the yard?

Roll a six. Then tap or click one of your highlighted yard tokens to place it on your starting square.

What happens when I land on another player's token?

On a non-safe track square, the opponent token is captured and returned to its yard. You also receive another roll.
