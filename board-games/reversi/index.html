Rules and controls

How to Play Reversi

Reversi is played on an 8×8 board with black and white discs. Black moves first. Your goal is to have more discs showing your color when neither player can make another legal move.

Find a legal square

Choose an empty highlighted square next to one or more white discs.

Trap the discs

Your new black disc must sandwich at least one white disc in a straight line.

Flip and continue

Every trapped disc flips to black. Turns continue until no legal moves remain.

When does a player pass?

If you have no legal move, your turn passes automatically. The game ends when both players have no move or the board is full.

A quick introduction

About the Reversi Game

Reversi is a two-player strategy game built around positional control. A single move can flip several lines of discs horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, so the lead can change quickly.

This browser version lets you play as Black against the computer. It runs entirely on your device and requires no download, account, or sign-up.

Reversi strategy tips

  • Prioritize corners because corner discs cannot be flipped.
  • Avoid giving away squares beside an open corner.
  • Keep your opponent’s legal move count low.
  • Do not chase an early lead; stable positions matter more.

Common questions

Reversi Game FAQ

Is Reversi the same as Othello?

The games use the same core flanking and disc-flipping mechanics. Othello is a trademarked commercial version with a standardized starting setup.

Can I play this Reversi game on mobile?

Yes. The board scales to fit smaller screens, and you can tap any highlighted legal move.

How do you win at Reversi?

You win by having more discs of your color when the game ends. Corners, stable edges, and limiting the opponent’s choices are usually more valuable than an early disc lead.

What happens when there are no legal moves?

A player with no legal move must pass. If neither player can move, the game ends and the discs are counted.
