How to Play Stunt Game

Accelerate across the hilly track and use ramps to get airborne. While your bike is in the air, use the rotation controls to complete flips. A clean landing keeps your run alive and adds stunt points.

  • Use Right Arrow to build speed.
  • Use Left Arrow to slow down before difficult landings.
  • Use Up and Down to rotate the bike while airborne.
  • Collect stars for bonus points.
  • Land with the bike aligned to the slope. A bad landing ends the run.

Stunt Game Scoring

Your score combines distance, collected stars, and successful flips. Longer runs and controlled stunt combinations produce higher scores. Your best score is saved locally in your browser, so you can try to beat it on your next run.

Tips for Better Stunts

  • Do not hold rotation constantly; release it once your bike is lined up for landing.
  • Approach large ramps with enough speed to clear the gap.
  • Brake slightly before a steep landing to reduce impact.
  • Watch the bike angle rather than only the front wheel.

About This Stunt Game

This lightweight browser stunt game uses HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript. It is designed for quick desktop and mobile play, with keyboard and touch controls and no account or download required.
