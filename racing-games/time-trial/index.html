How to Play Time Trial

Time Trial is a simple browser racing game built around speed, control, and route accuracy. Your car automatically starts on the circuit. Accelerate through corners, pass the glowing checkpoints in order, and complete three laps while time remains.

  • Use WASD or the arrow keys to drive.
  • Steer smoothly instead of holding a turn for too long.
  • Drive through checkpoints in their numbered order.
  • Touch the mobile controls on smaller screens.
  • Finish all three laps to win the time trial.

Time Trial Controls

Accelerate W / ↑ Brake / Reverse S / ↓ Steer A / D or ← / → Mobile Touch controls

Scoring and Game States

Every completed checkpoint awards points, while finishing a lap gives a larger bonus. A clean, fast run is rewarded because your remaining time is converted into a finish bonus. If the clock reaches zero before the final lap, the race ends. Your fastest completed race is saved locally in your browser.

Tips for a Faster Time

  • Start accelerating early on long straight sections.
  • Brake before a tight corner and accelerate as you exit it.
  • Keep the car near the middle of the road when approaching a turn.
  • Use the checkpoints as visual markers for learning the racing line.

About Time Trial Game

This free Time Trial game is designed for quick browser sessions. There is no download, account, database, or server-side processing required. Your best completed time is stored only in your browser using local storage.

Time Trial FAQ

Is Time Trial free to play?

Yes. The game runs directly in a modern web browser and does not require a download.

Can I play Time Trial on mobile?

Yes. Touch controls are provided for acceleration, braking, and steering.

How do I improve my Time Trial score?

Hit checkpoints cleanly, maintain speed on straights, and slow down only as much as necessary for corners.
