How to Play Drift

Your car moves forward automatically. Steer left and right to follow the road. When a corner approaches, hold Drift while steering to slide around the bend. The longer you maintain a controlled slide, the more your combo grows.

About Drift Game

This browser drift game is built around quick arcade handling: automatic acceleration, responsive steering, controlled slides, combo scoring, and increasingly demanding corners.

Scoring

Distance adds points. A successful drift banks bonus points based on its duration and your current combo multiplier.

Drift Game FAQ

Can I play Drift Game on mobile?

Yes. Use the large left, Drift, and right touch controls below the game canvas.

No. The game runs in a modern web browser using HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript.

Keep the car on the road, maintain longer controlled drifts, and avoid breaking your combo with a crash.
