ABOUT THE GAME

A quick world geography challenge

Geography Quiz is a browser-based educational game for practicing general world geography. It is designed for short sessions, so you can replay a round whenever you want to test your memory.

There is no download or account required. The quiz runs entirely in your browser and works on desktop and mobile screens.

Geography Quiz Tips

  • Look for clues in the wording of the question before choosing.
  • Use continent and regional knowledge to narrow down unfamiliar answers.
  • Keep a steady pace: speed matters, but an accurate answer is worth more than a rushed guess.

Geography Game FAQ

Is the Geography Quiz free to play?

Yes. The game is designed to be played for free in a modern web browser with no account or download required.

Can I play the Geography Quiz on a phone?

Yes. The answer choices are touch-friendly and the game layout adapts to smaller screens.

How does scoring work?

A correct answer awards base points, with an additional streak bonus for consecutive correct answers. Faster correct answers also earn a small time bonus.

What topics are included?

The question set includes countries, capitals, continents, oceans, landmarks, borders, and physical geography.
