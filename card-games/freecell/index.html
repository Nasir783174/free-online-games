How to Play FreeCell

FreeCell uses one standard 52-card deck. All cards are dealt face up into eight cascades, so every card is visible from the start. Your goal is to move all cards to the four foundation piles.

  1. Build foundations upward by suit, starting with Ace and ending with King.
  2. Build cascade columns downward in alternating colors.
  3. Move one exposed card into any empty free cell.
  4. Use empty cascades and free cells to temporarily store cards and unlock longer sequences.
  5. Move every card to the foundations to win.

FreeCell Controls

  • Desktop: Click a card or valid sequence, then click a destination.
  • Mobile: Tap a card or sequence, then tap a free cell, foundation, or cascade.
  • Double-click / double-tap: Sends an eligible exposed card to its foundation.
  • Restart Deal: Resets the current shuffled deal.
  • New Game: Creates a fresh shuffled deal.

About FreeCell

FreeCell is an open-information solitaire game because every card starts face up. Unlike many luck-heavy solitaire variants, FreeCell emphasizes planning, temporary storage, sequencing, and careful use of empty spaces.

FreeCell Strategy Tips

  • Keep at least one free cell open when possible.
  • Expose low cards early so Aces and Twos can reach the foundations.
  • Empty a cascade only when you have a clear plan for how to use it.
  • Do not rush every available card to the foundation if it may block an important alternating sequence.

FreeCell FAQ

Is this FreeCell game free to play?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser and does not require a download, account, database, or backend.

Can I play FreeCell on mobile?

Yes. The layout adapts to smaller screens and supports tap-based card selection and movement.

How many free cells are there?

There are four free cells. Each can temporarily hold one exposed card.

How do I win FreeCell?

Move all 52 cards to the four suit foundations in ascending order from Ace through King.
