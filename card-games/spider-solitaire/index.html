How to Play Spider Solitaire

The goal is to remove all 104 cards from the tableau. Arrange cards in descending rank, then complete full same-suit sequences from King through Ace. Every completed run is automatically removed from play.

  1. Build downward: place a lower-ranked card or sequence onto the next higher rank, such as a 9 onto a 10.
  2. Move packed sequences: you can move several cards together only when every card in that group is descending and all cards share the same suit.
  3. Use empty columns: any single card or valid movable sequence can be placed into an empty tableau column.
  4. Deal new rows: when every column contains at least one card, use the stock to deal one face-up card onto each column.
  5. Clear eight runs: remove eight complete King-to-Ace same-suit runs to win the game.

Spider Solitaire Controls

  • Desktop: click a face-up card to select it, then click the destination column.
  • Mobile: tap a card, then tap where you want the sequence to move.
  • Deal: click or tap the stock pile when no tableau column is empty.
  • Restart: select a difficulty and choose New Game.

Spider Solitaire Tips

  • Prioritize exposing face-down cards before making cosmetic moves.
  • Create empty columns because they provide the most flexible temporary storage.
  • Keep same-suit cards together whenever possible so larger sequences stay movable.
  • Avoid dealing from the stock until you have improved the current tableau as much as possible.

About Spider Solitaire

Spider Solitaire is a two-deck patience game built around long descending sequences. Unlike classic Klondike, Spider lets you stack cards by rank regardless of suit, but only a fully ordered same-suit sequence can move as a group. The one-suit mode is the most approachable, while two-suit and four-suit games require more planning.

Spider Solitaire FAQ

Is this Spider Solitaire game free?

Yes. The game runs directly in your browser and does not require a download, login, or account.

Can I play Spider Solitaire on mobile?

Yes. The tableau is responsive, and the game uses tap-based controls that work on modern touch devices.

What is the difference between 1-suit, 2-suit, and 4-suit Spider Solitaire?

The rules stay the same, but adding more suits makes it harder to create movable same-suit sequences and complete King-to-Ace runs.

Why can’t I deal a new row?

Standard Spider Solitaire does not allow a stock deal while any tableau column is empty. Move a card or sequence into the empty column first.
